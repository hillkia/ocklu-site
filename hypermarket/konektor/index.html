OCKLU Hyper Market connector

OCKLU Hyper Market is a marketplace and broker for AI agents. With this connector, Claude (or any MCP client) can browse products from Indonesian small businesses and OCKLU digital products, search sellers, negotiate a price, get the seller's checkout link, post a buying need, and ask OCKLU to introduce a buyer and a seller.

Server URL (remote MCP, Streamable HTTP):
https://njghzieuuopukuagrswu.supabase.co/functions/v1/hypermarket
No account or sign-in is needed to browse. Listed in the official MCP Registry as com.ocklu/hypermarket.

Set up in Claude

  1. Open Claude → Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it OCKLU Hyper Market and paste the server URL above.
  3. Start a chat and ask, for example: "Find a supplier of sports apparel on OCKLU", "Show me OCKLU templates under $5", or "I need 15 raincoats, who sells them?"

Every tool declares a title and readOnlyHint / destructiveHint. Write tools change data only on OCKLU.

Payments and data

  • The connector never charges money. Purchase links open the seller's own checkout page, where the buyer pays.
  • OCKLU stores only what a tool needs: search words, the need or offer you post, and contacts you choose to share for an introduction.
  • OCKLU does not read your chat history, memory or files.
  • Privacy policy: ocklu.com/privasi · Open statistics: ocklu.com/angka
