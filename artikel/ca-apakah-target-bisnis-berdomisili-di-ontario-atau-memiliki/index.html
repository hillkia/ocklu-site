Extra-Provincial Registration Triggers Under the Ontario Business Corporations Act

Extra-provincial registration is not a second incorporation, but rather the host province's mechanism for granting a corporation permission to "carry on business" within its borders [6]. A corporation incorporated in another Canadian province or federally must register as an extra-provincial corporation when conducting operations in Ontario [6, 7]. Furthermore, foreign corporations wishing to operate in Ontario must obtain a license under the Extra-Provincial Corporations Act (EPCA) [7].

The operational threshold for triggering these compliance requirements is notably broad and often catches founders off guard [6].
