This reliance stems directly from a structural crisis: the emergence of approximately 6 million people aged 60 or older defined as "people with a shopping handicap," concentrated heavily in depopulated rural districts and aging urban fringe communities [7]. As the demographic pyramid inverts, the konbini format assumes a multifunctional service role to sustain basic daily survival [7].

To cope with severe labor shortages and an escalating super-aging population, physical retail layouts across Japan are undergoing forced technological retrofitting rather than wholesale reconstruction [10]. This dual approach demonstrates that physical infrastructure must aggressively automate and adapt to serve an immobile consumer base safely.
