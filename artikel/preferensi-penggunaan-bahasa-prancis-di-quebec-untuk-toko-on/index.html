Introduction to the EU Web Accessibility Directive and BFSG Enforcement

The European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) is a landmark EU directive mandating accessibility standards for consumer-facing digital products and services across all member states [5]. While the directive required transposition by June 2022, its enforcement officially commenced on June 28, 2025 [5].

In Germany, the directive has been implemented nationally through the Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz (BFSG) [11]. This legislation extends strict accessibility requirements—referencing EN 301 549 and WCAG 2.1 Level AA benchmarks—directly to the private sector, encompassing e-commerce websites, banking platforms, transport booking services, and digital media [11].

To enforce compliance, Germany’s implementation establishes a framework of administrative sanctions managed by federal and Länder-level market surveillance authorities (Marktüberwachungsbehörden) [11]. Statutory penalties for non-compliance under the German implementation include potential administrative fines reaching up to EUR 100,000 [11].

The legislation specifically targets commercial digital products and e-commerce platforms, though it provides defined exemptions for micro-enterprises with fewer than 10 employees and an annual turnover below EUR 2 million, unless they operate public-facing services [11].
