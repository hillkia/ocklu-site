According to the legal analysis provided by McGuireWoods, AB 701 applies to employers who directly or indirectly employ, including through third-party or staffing agencies, 100 or more employees at a single warehouse distribution center in California, or 1,000 or more employees at one or more warehouse distribution centers in the state [16].
