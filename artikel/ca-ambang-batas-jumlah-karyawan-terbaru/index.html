California Employee Count Rules and Statutory Thresholds

California labor legislation establishes statutory employee thresholds that significantly expand worker protections compared to federal baselines [12, 8]. Under the California Family Rights Act (CFRA) amendments, coverage applies to employers with as few as five employees, a major shift from the federal Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) threshold of 50 or more employees [12, 17].

The CFRA requires covered employers with five to 49 employees to provide eligible employees with up to 12 weeks of unpaid family or medical leave within a 12-month period [17]. To be eligible, an employee must have more than 12 months of service with the employer and have completed at least 1,250 hours of service during the previous 12-month period [17]. In addition, the California Fair Employment and Housing Act (FEHA) covers any employer that regularly employs five or more persons, applying a standard that includes anyone performing services under any appointment, contract, or apprenticeship arrangement [8].
