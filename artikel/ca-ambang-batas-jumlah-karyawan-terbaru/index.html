California Pay Data Reporting Thresholds and Headcount Realities

Under California labor law, the California Civil Rights Department (CRD) mandates annual pay data reporting for private employers based on strict employee count thresholds [12, 13]. Specifically, a Payroll Employee Report is triggered if an employer has 100 or more payroll employees total, anywhere in the United States, provided that at least one of those employees works within California [12].

Similarly, a separate Labor Contractor Employee Report is required if an employer has 100 or more workers hired through labor contractors, with at least one such worker located in California [12].

Crucially, the 100-employee requirement is calculated using total headcount rather than being restricted to California-based staff [12]. For example, an organization employing 95 workers in Texas and 10 workers in California crosses the statutory threshold and is legally obligated to file [12]. When an employer meets both criteria, they must submit two distinct reports, each adhering to its own specific template and field structure [12].
