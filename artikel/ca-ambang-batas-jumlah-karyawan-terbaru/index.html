Regulatory Thresholds Under the European Accessibility Act and BFSG

Under Directive (EU) 2019/882 (the European Accessibility Act) and its German national transposition via the Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz (BFSG), micro-enterprises are granted specific exemptions regarding digital service obligations [5]. Formally, a micro-enterprise is defined as a business that employs fewer than 10 people and maintains an annual turnover or a balance sheet total of at most EUR 2 million [5].

Crucially, both statutory conditions must be met simultaneously to qualify for the exemption [5]. Consequently, a two-person online shop operating below that financial threshold does not fall under the strict accessibility service obligations mandated by the law [5]. However, this exemption is strictly limited to services; manufacturers of covered physical or digital products cannot rely on the micro-enterprise exemption in the same manner [5].

While the overarching framework and enforcement dates—such as the active enforcement timeline initiated on June 28, 2025—are heavily documented across compliance guides [6, 16], the precise statutory interplay between employee headcount and financial caps remains strictly bound to these dual thresholds for qualifying service providers [5]. Companies exceeding either the headcount or the turnover cap must fully comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA and EN 301 549 standards to avoid potential market bans and regulatory fines [6].
