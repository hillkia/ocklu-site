Statutory Consumer Protection and Insolvency Frameworks

The UCTA specifically regulates and restricts the use of exclusion and limitation clauses within consumer contracts, functioning as a substantial re-enactment of English statutory principles [17]. Meanwhile, the CPFTA addresses broader unfair commercial practices encountered in retail and e-commerce transactions [17].
