Introduction to Government Standard Adoption

Public policy distinguishes sharply between voluntary standards and mandatory technical regulations. While general standardization provides a baseline for quality, the government actively deploys compulsory implementation through ministerial decrees to govern critical economic sectors [12, 17]. For instance, the Ministry of Industry enforced mandatory Indonesian National Standards (SNI) via Regulation No. 75/2024 for specific audio, video, and electronic devices [11], and subsequently enacted Regulation No. 22/2026 to mandate SNI compliance for safety glass used in buildings and panels [12].

These mandatory frameworks serve a dual public policy function: safeguarding public security, health, and consumer safety against substandard materials, while simultaneously protecting the domestic market and enhancing the competitiveness of local industries [12].
