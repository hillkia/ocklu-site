This framework operates alongside national initiatives such as the Enabling Masterplan 2030, which targets full accessibility compliance for high-traffic government websites [6, 12]. While public sector digital services are bound by mandatory standards like Instruction Manual 8 (IM8) and the Singapore Government Design System (SGDS) [5], the application of these standards to private e-commerce differs significantly.
