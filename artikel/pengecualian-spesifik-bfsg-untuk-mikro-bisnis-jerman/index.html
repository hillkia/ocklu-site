Regulatory Scope and Micro-Enterprise Definition Under the German BFSG

The Accessibility Strengthening Act (BFSG) serves as Germany's national implementation of the European Accessibility Act (EAA), officially entering into force on June 28, 2025 [14, 20]. The legislation establishes binding accessibility requirements across significant segments of the private sector for digital products and services, aiming to facilitate equal participation for individuals with disabilities and older persons [20].

Regarding exemptions, § 2 Nr. 17 of the BFSG outlines specific relief provisions for micro-enterprises by referencing EU Recommendation 2003/361/EC [15]. To qualify for this exemption, a business must simultaneously satisfy strict dual criteria: it must maintain fewer than 10 employees (calculated in full-time equivalents) AND report an annual turnover or a balance sheet total that does not exceed 2 million euros [15].

Failing to meet either metric strips the enterprise of its exempt status [15]. Furthermore, the scope of this regulatory protection remains narrow; the exemption applies exclusively to micro-enterprises that provide services, whereas physical or digital products sold by micro-enterprises remain fully bound by the accessibility mandates of the BFSG [15].
