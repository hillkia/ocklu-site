California Compliance Thresholds and Current Employee Count Status

Under California Government Code section 12999, private employers are mandated to submit annual pay data reports to the California Civil Rights Department (CRD) [11, 12, 13]. For the reporting year 2025—with filings due by May 13, 2026—the exact current employee count threshold is determined by total aggregate headcount rather than state-specific numbers [11, 12].

Two distinct categories dictate compliance obligations under this mandate:

  • Payroll Employee Report: Required for any private employer with a total of 100 or more payroll employees anywhere in the U.S., provided at least one of those employees works within California [11].
  • Labor Contractor Employee Report: Required for private client employers utilizing 100 or more workers hired through labor contractors, with at least one such worker operating in California [11, 12].

The 100-employee threshold relies strictly on total enterprise headcount [11]. For example, an organization maintaining 95 workers in another state and 10 workers in California clears the statutory threshold and must file [11]. Employers meeting both criteria must submit two separate reports, each utilizing distinct templates and field structures designated by the CRD [11].
