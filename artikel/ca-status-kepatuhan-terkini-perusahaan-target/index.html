The Gap Between Regulatory Expectation and Available Evidence

The conflict between regulatory precision and data accessibility is stark. Employment laws are not applied uniformly; instead, specific federal regulations trigger automatically when a workforce reaches defined headcounts, such as 15, 20, or 50 employees [5]. These thresholds dictate immediate changes in anti-discrimination, leave policies, and benefits compliance [5]. However, determining a company’s exact position relative to these triggers requires verifiable payroll data, which is often opaque to external stakeholders.

While audit confirmations are designed to provide independent evidence by interacting directly with third parties, such as banks, to mitigate the risk of internally generated or edited documents [13], this method does not easily translate to verifying real-time employee counts against statutory thresholds.
