02

The one rule

Everything else on this page follows from it.

Scripts beat feelings. Every time.

12K – 31K views

  • Big title card at the top of the frame, visible at second zero
  • Gives words a mom can copy tonight — "Walking feet, please"
  • Numbered, each point a concrete action
  • Worth saving → earns saves and shares

1K – 3.2K views

  • Small centered text, no title
  • Gives a feeling — "You're doing better than you think"
  • Essay monologue, nothing to take away
  • Nothing to save → a like, then gone

Your writing isn't the problem

"I am the table" is the best-written thing on your grid. It got 2,773 views. People don't share compliments about themselves — they share solutions they can use. Keep writing those posts; just cap them at one a week and give the other six something to steal.

Your 30K post wasn't a parenting post

"Work: 9–5. Daycare: 8–3. School: 8–3… It's not an organizational problem. It's a structural problem." That's economics, dressed as rage. And on @regulatewithkia the exact same structure — "1980 vs 2026: side income didn't need one / almost feels necessary" — is your highest-intent post there.

Same template, two accounts, two audiences. It's the only format that should cross between them.

06

Where people go

Right now the path is: post → view → nothing.

Cold
A script reel or a systems rant on @kiamomjourney. 15–30K strangers. Costs them nothing to watch.
Warm
Follow and save — triggered by a carousel worth keeping and a profile that promises something.
Captured
DM keyword. This is the missing step. "Comment HIT → I'll send the scripts." A DM is a real contact, not a view.
Yours
Newsletter, via the free 30 Calm-Down Scripts. An audience you own rather than rent from Meta.
The room
@regulatewithkia and the Skool community. 17.6% engagement — this is where warm people belong.

Don't send cold traffic straight to the community

At 4,634 followers, strangers don't join rooms. DM first, email second, community third. If one 30K reel earns 300 keyword comments and 40% convert, that's roughly 120 subscribers from a single post. That number is currently zero — only because nothing is set up to catch them.
