How the squad heals.

1 · Scan

Finds every server, port and cron/launchd job. Uses the port that is actually open, not the one written in a config.

2 · Diagnose

Calls only the APIs your own page calls when it loads — nothing a browser wouldn't do. Sorts failures: network, quota, key, port clash, or real code.

3 · Nurse

Restarts what died, reruns jobs once the network is back, and never touches anything that needs your credentials.

4 · Doctor (Claude)

Backup → smallest possible fix (no shell, no web, no .env) → retest. Score up = keep and learn a rule. Otherwise = exact rollback.

5 · Learn

Every keep or rollback becomes a written rule the doctor reads next night. Your own notes are read first.

6 · Phone

A cloud guard keeps checking your public pages and cron jobs even when your laptop is off, and pings you on Telegram.
