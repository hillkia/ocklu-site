Scope: the homepage only. This audit reads three files — indielee.com, its robots.txt and its llms.txt. It does not crawl the rest of the site. So “no FAQPage schema” here means not on the homepage; a dedicated FAQ page may well have it, and several audited stores do. That distinction matters and it was wrong to leave it implied.
