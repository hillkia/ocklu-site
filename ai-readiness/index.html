Which e-commerce sites can AI assistants actually read?

26 sites checked · updated 2026-08-17 · free, no signup

Two different things get confused constantly: whether an AI assistant is allowed to read a site, and whether it has enough structure to quote it confidently. This page measures both, separately.

0 of 26 sites here block a major AI crawler in robots.txt. Outright blocking gets talked about a lot; in this sample it simply is not happening.

The gap is elsewhere. 23 of 26 sites have fewer than two headings phrased the way buyers actually ask a question, and 5 score below 60 overall — mostly for missing FAQ or Product structured data. An assistant that is allowed in still has to find something quotable once it arrives.
