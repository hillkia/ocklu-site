Which e-commerce sites can AI assistants actually read?

125 sites checked · updated 2026-08-25 · free, no signup

Two different things get confused constantly: whether an AI assistant is allowed to read a site, and whether it has enough structure to quote it confidently. This page measures both, separately.

2 of 125 sites here block a major AI crawler in robots.txt. For those, nothing else on this page matters until that line is removed.

The gap is elsewhere. 110 of 125 sites have fewer than two headings phrased the way buyers actually ask a question, and 20 score below 60 overall — mostly for missing FAQ or Product structured data. An assistant that is allowed in still has to find something quotable once it arrives.
